# Demo script (about 2 minutes)

Prove three things: the AI personalizes, the teacher can start calm from across the room, and both adults are kept in the loop.

| Time | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | The problem: a teacher with a full class cannot always reach one student in time, and one calming routine does not fit every kid. |
| 0:20–0:50 | The assessment: a parent fills in the form; the AI profile and calm-screen preview appear; they tap Approve. |
| 0:50–1:25 | **Key shot, one unedited take:** split screen of the teacher's phone and the student's watch. Teacher taps Start calm; within seconds the watch shows the breathing circle, vibrates and plays the sound. The parent's phone shows the alert. |
| 1:25–1:45 | Backup triggers: a teammate rocks back and forth (or the room gets loud); the watch starts on its own and the teacher's page lights up. |
| 1:45–2:00 | Close: two profiles side by side (a sound-sensitive kid vs a kid who loves space). |

## Talking points
- Runs on one off-the-shelf watch: no extra sensors to wear.
- Works offline for the student tap, motion and noise; teacher commands resume when WiFi returns.
- Never records audio; parents approve every AI profile.
- Built to COPPA's rules: parent consent, minimal data, delete button.
- A support tool alongside teachers and therapists, not a medical device.
