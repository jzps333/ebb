# Ebb demo video (~2 min)

Each scene: what's on screen, then the narration line (the calm "Sarah" voice).

1. **Title** — Ebb logo on cream, sky gradient.
   "For many kids with special needs, a meltdown builds long before anyone notices. By the time an adult steps in, the moment has already escalated."

2. **Watch: sensing** — watch mockup, agitation bar rising green to amber.
   "Ebb is a calming smartwatch that notices first. It learns each child's own normal, from movement like rocking or hand-flapping, and from how loud the room is. It never records audio."

3. **Watch: tiers** — breathing buzz cue, then a real calm scene with breathing circle.
   "When agitation rises, Ebb starts with a gentle breathing buzz on the wrist. If it keeps rising, calm mode begins on its own, with a scene the child loves and a familiar, soothing voice."

4. **Watch: check-in** — three faces.
   "Afterwards, the child taps how they feel."

5. **App: live** — Live agitation screen.
   "Teachers and parents see it live. If a child still isn't settling, Ebb alerts the nearest adult."

6. **App: learning** — moment with outcome + false alarm, sensitivity log.
   "And every moment teaches Ebb something. It measures how much agitation actually dropped. A teacher's rating tunes its sensitivity, so false alarms fade over time."

7. **App: insights** — heatmap, scene effectiveness bands, risk forecast, AI report.
   "It learns which scene helps most at each time of day, forecasts the riskiest hours ahead, and writes a weekly report with ideas to try."

8. **Texting** — iMessage thread.
   "Parents and teachers can simply text Ebb: how was today? Notes are shared between home and school."

9. **Setup** — pairing QR, calm profile, voice, weekly scenes.
   "Setup takes minutes: scan the watch, answer a few questions, and approve a calm profile, a voice, and this week's scenes."

10. **Close** — privacy line + logo.
   "Nicknames only. No audio. Location only if parents opt in. Ebb doesn't replace the adults. It helps them get there sooner."
